# RESULTS: what the multi-seed controls say about membrane-propulsion

Generated by `python3 rigor.py --write` (stdlib only, seeded, deterministic) from `results/results.json`. Do not edit by hand; run `python3 rigor.py --render-only` to rebuild.

**What this is.** A synthetic toy: a Kuramoto phase ring, a travelling drive, and a probe whose velocity is *defined* by an assumed law (`v = mu * local phase gradient`). Nothing here is propulsion, thrust, force, energy, or a physical space drive, and no physical claim is made. "Displacement" is a displacement of a simulated probe along ring sites, in site units.

**Headline, honestly.** The sign and size of the probe displacement under a locked drive are **by construction** (probe law x locked phase gradient). What is *emergent* is only the locking itself, its threshold, and what the ring does when it is not driven or is noisy. The most important control is the **no-drive ring**: it drifts on its own in a large fraction of seeds, so single-seed numbers (including the README's) must not be read as "the drive causes the drift" without that control.

### Anatomy: why the ring drifts without a drive

A twisted state (nonzero integer winding number w) is a stable fixed pattern of the ring and relaxes only very slowly (estimated relaxation time ~ N^2/(pi^2 K) = 208 time units, versus a default warmup of 10). The probe law then gives drift = w*mu*T. The default "steady state" is therefore partly a transient.

Reading: the circular interpolation handles the +-pi wrap and the ring seam correctly (the naive linear average would be wrong by up to pi at wrap-straddling positions). k_est and coherence are position-independent for a locked wave (by construction, uniform gradient), and omega is -Omega at every position. **Weakness found:** `omega` is the phase difference between the last two samples divided by dt, so if the sensor's `position` is changed between samples the result is a spatial difference mislabeled as a frequency (above: +14.71 instead of -1). This is a usage caveat, not a bug in the stated design (the sensor is described as fixed).

## 11. Falsified, surprising, and negative results

- The v0.2.0 upload's statement "seed 3 gives -28.1" (README, DESIGN, demo, a test comment) was wrong: it is seed 2 (seed 3 gives -1.0). The label is corrected in this PR.
- The no-drive ring is not "approximately still": 32% of seeds drift >= 10 sites and 14% drift as far as the driven ring or farther (max 98.1 sites). The no-drive control must be run over many seeds before any drive-vs-no-drive claim.
- The default warmup (10 time units) is far shorter than the twisted-state relaxation time (~208); the "steady-state" no-drive number at default settings is a transient, and twisted states persist even after long warmup.
- Noise: at sigma = 2 the sign is retained in only 57.0% (57/100; Wilson 95% 47.2-66.3%) of seeds.
- dt = 0.4 gives wrong (non-converged) driven numbers; no-drive drifts are not converged at the default dt (mean |difference to the finest dt| 1.54 sites).
- Sensor coherence cannot distinguish a driven from an undriven ring (AUC 0.58), and under noise it ranks noisy driven rings below undriven ones.
- Single-site k_est under noise is unreliable, and on twisted no-drive rings it is a local gradient and does not equal the winding.
- Sensor omega is meaningless if the position changes between samples and aliases when the sampling interval exceeds pi/Omega.
- Nothing here supports any physical interpretation: the direction control is the assumed probe law (probe-law null: zero law gives zero, constant law gives drive-independent motion).

## 12. Honest limits

- Synthetic toy. Not propulsion, not thrust, not force, not energy, not a space drive. No physics is claimed or implied.
- The probe law is **assumed**, not derived. All displacement results follow from it.
- One model, one parameter family (N=64, K=2, A=1.5, Omega=1, omega0=0, dt=0.05) with sweeps around it; Euler / Euler-Maruyama integration only.
- Seeds are pseudo-random streams (zlib.crc32-tagged); 200 seeds for the main controls, fewer (10-100) for sweeps; intervals are Wilson (proportions) and percentile bootstrap (5000 resamples).
- No claim about any hardware, any medium, or any application.
